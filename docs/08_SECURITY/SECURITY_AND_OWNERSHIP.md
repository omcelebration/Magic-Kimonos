# SECURITY AND OWNERSHIP

## Correct access model
Invite the agent/developer through Shopify Users or collaborator access. Do not share the owner's password.

## Principle of least privilege
Grant only what is needed. Remove or reduce access after the work is complete.

## Owner-only actions
Keep these owner-controlled unless absolutely necessary:
- billing;
- payouts and banking;
- ownership transfer;
- user administration;
- domain transfer;
- payment provider changes;
- deletion of the store;
- legal identity information.

## Secrets
Store secrets only in approved secret managers or platform environment variables. Never:
- commit them to Git;
- paste them into public chats;
- include them in screenshots;
- put them in theme Liquid;
- place them in this ZIP.

## Publishing
The agent works on an unpublished theme. The owner approves the preview. Preserve a rollback theme and Git commit.

## Customer data
Only access or export customer/order data when operationally required. Follow applicable privacy law and minimize copies.

## Asset rights
Do not publish third-party images, fonts, music, artwork, or logos without confirmed rights.
